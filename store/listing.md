Come home to Kagaribi, the village of the hidden ember.

THEN EARN YOUR WAY NORTH

THREE HEROES, SIX ELEMENTS

## Names and search words

**"Naruto" is deliberately not in any store text.** Both stores forbid using another brand's name to rank:
Google Play's Metadata and Intellectual Property policies, and Apple's App Review Guideline 2.3.7 (no
trademarked terms or other apps' names in metadata) and 5.2.1. The usual results are a rejected listing, a
removed app, and on Google a strike against the developer account. A phrase like "Naruto inspired" is caught
by the same rules, and it also hands the rights holder written proof that the game leans on their brand.

What ranks instead, and is allowed, is the genre language players actually type. It is already worked into
the texts above: **ninja, shinobi, anime, jutsu, hand signs, ninja village, ninja academy, ramen**.

**The title.** "Will of Fire" is a phrase strongly tied to that same series (it is the name of one of its
films). It is your call and it is used everywhere now, but together with the names Tachi and Susake it makes
a takedown complaint more likely. The cheapest protection is to rename those two characters before going
public (`src/main.js`, `src/story.js`).
