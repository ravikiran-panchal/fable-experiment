**Notes for review** (paste into App Review Information)

> Single-player offline game. No account or sign-in is needed. The camera permission is optional: it is
> requested only when the player presses "Weave with your camera" in the Sign Weave panel (press V in game),
> to recognise hand signs on the device. Frames are never recorded, stored or sent. Everything can be played
> without the camera using the on-screen sign pad (keys 1 to 6). The app makes no network requests.
> Controls: WASD move, Space jump, J strike, V sign weave, E interact.
